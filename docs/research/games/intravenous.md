# Intravenous series (Explosive Squat Games): systems and presentation reference

Researched 2026-11-10. Purpose: reference for our side-view three.js infiltration game (stealth or loud, shootable and switchable lights, vents, cameras, alarms, noise, alert states, radios, fire, smoke, flashbangs). The owner named Intravenous 2 and 3 as the target vibe ("grungy cyberpunk" and its systems).

Source labels used throughout: **[API]** = Steam store API / Steam news API text (developer posts and patch notes, read in full); **[PCGN]** = PCGamesN article actually opened; **[PCG]** = PC Gamer article actually opened; **[EG]** = egamers.io review actually opened; **[SNIP]** = only a search-result snippet or a Steam news feed teaser, not the full article; **[SHOT]** = I looked at the screenshot myself; **[COMM]** = community Steam guide (opened, weak for mechanics). Full URLs are in section 12.

## 1. Stealth

### 1.1 Light and darkness

Facts from the developer:

- Store page IV1: "Your visibility depends on how well-lit the environment is, and every step you take makes noise." [API]
- IV2 devlog "Under the Guise of Darkness" (1 Dec 2023): the HUD shows your **light level** and your **noise level**. Darkness does not make you invisible: "your level of illumination determines how far away enemies will see you and how quickly they'll spot you". In a very dark place you are only guaranteed undetected unless "within centimeters of the enemy". [API]
- Going prone greatly reduces the distance at which enemies can always detect you in very dark areas: "if you're in a very dark area, enemies will only be able to detect you if they bump into you". Enemies will also trip over a prone, barely visible player (IV2 devlog [API]; "causing enemies to fall by bumping into you while prone is now slightly easier", IV2 1.2.8 [API]).
- Light level calculation (IV1 1.4.0, backported from IV2): the game now evaluates the **grayscale value of the light rather than the sum average**, so "dark areas are now darker, while bright areas remain just as bright, if not brighter". Coloured but dim light therefore lights you up less. [API]
- IV2 1.2.0: "added a silhouette effect for the player when he is in a dark area, to ensure better awareness of the player's position in near-pitch-black darkness areas". [API]
- IV2 1.4.6: the player's visual height value used for lighting was wrong, which made the player seem unlit where lit. Lighting has a **height** concept: obstacles give cover from lamps ("obstacles in one of the spots on Decrepit Industrial Area not providing sufficient cover from lamps", IV2 1.4.0). [API] IV3:B 1.1.15 mentions "depth-aware lighting" that reduces halos around objects (1.1.0, 1.1.1). [API]
- Bushes hide the player and slow movement ("bush movement speed influence"; "bushes now better hide the player", Borderline 1.3.0). [API]
- Prone also lowers height: in Borderline "throwing a throwable briefly set the player height to standing, so you could be spotted although prone behind an obstacle" was a bug fixed in 1.1.15. [API]
- IV3 prone redesign (Devlog #2, 7 Aug 2026): prone previously "meant reducing your visibility and breaking lines of sight at the expense of total vulnerability"; now **all weapons and most gadgets can be used while prone**, e.g. the HSR-EMP without standing up. Reloading prone commits you to finish the reload. Vaulting over low objects and climbing over tall ones now works for most objects, not just designated walls. [API]

**Switching lights off.**
- You can shoot lamps out, flip **light switches** and **circuit breakers/breaker boxes** (store page IV1; PCGamesN preview "meddle with the fuse box around the side instead and draw a guard out to fix the problem, allowing you to either deal with them directly or sneak through in the darkness" [PCGN prologue article]).
- A breaker box controls a set of lamps per building; the dev spent many patches fixing "light not controlled by a breaker box" per level (IV2 1.1-1.4), which shows each lamp has an explicit controller link in the map data. Breaker boxes cannot be added to security cameras or burning barrels (IV2 1.2.11). [API]
- Enemies **react to lights going out**: "fixed goons not reacting to lights being switched off via breaker boxes correctly" and "goons indefinitely trying to turn on broken breaker boxes" / "not going to re-enable breaker boxes" (IV2 1.3.2) [API]; goons "remarking about how dark the surrounding area is" and going to flip light switches, but not for indoor lamps while outside (IV2 1.4.0) [API]; Borderline 1.1.15: "fixed goons not reacting to lights being switched on by the player"; 1.1.19/1.2.0: goons "avoid smoke" logic can be interrupted by flipping light switches (bug). A screenshot shows the enemy line "Damn lights..." [SHOT bd_2]. IV2 1.3.15: "lamps flickering if broken while the light switch is turned off" (bug fix) shows broken lamps flicker/spark. [API]
- **EMP**: the HSR-EMP (aimed device) and PKC-EMP (area around player) disrupt electrical devices: lamps, cameras, smoke detectors, sprinklers, always-on cars are excluded (Borderline 1.2.0 "fixed being able to aim at undisruptable objects with HSR-EMP (for example, always-on cars)"). HSR-EMP shows a small wave effect on the disrupted object (IV1 1.4.0). In Borderline 1.3.0: "can disrupt lamps that have already been disrupted again", "HSR-EMP and PKC-EMP now regenerate their charge while dropped on the floor", "HSR-EMP can now be modified". PKC-EMP "recharges quickly only when moving" and its weight penalises movement noise and speed (IV1 1.4.0). [API]
- **Multi-Launcher** (IV2): pneumatic rifle in the primary slot with rubber slugs, EMP charges (disrupt electrical objects) and gas pellets, with a heavy noise and movement penalty. [API]

**Flashlights.** Store page IV1: "use flashlights in darkness". Flashlight cones, security camera cones and the player view cone have soft fade-in edges (IV1 1.4.0 backport). Custom difficulty can randomise flashlights; reinforcement spawns now get flashlights and radios "where fitting" (IV2 1.4.0). The cone is the readable threat in screenshots (blue-white beams). [API][SHOT]

**Night vision.** NVGs exist (toggle; IV1 1.4.0 made them sharper with "more neutral palette rather than a strong green tint"). Screens show green grain and heavy bloom on bright lights [SHOT iv1_8, iv2_5]. Borderline 1.1.17: NVG visuals bugged if a save made with NVG on was loaded (the state is saved). [API]

**Line-of-sight indicator.** The player has an LOS cone/line indicator, with options "regular / limited (only when aiming or when an obstacle is between you and the aim point) / minimal (only when aiming or with a laser pointer)" (IV2 1.2.0). Laser pointers and lasers raise your visibility level (Borderline 1.1.17 fix "laser-sight equipped & enabled weapons raise the player's visibility level"). Fog of War mutator hides enemies out of view and slightly darkens out-of-view areas (IV2 1.1 and 1.2.0). [API]

### 1.2 Sound

- Both a **noise meter** and light meter sit in the HUD (IV2 devlog). Loadout screens show "Noise factor" and "Mobility" for the whole kit (screenshot: noise factor 192%, mobility 84.2% [SHOT iv2_6]). The pause menu and inventory screen show the same values (Borderline 1.1.0 fix "mismatch in the noise and mobility levels between the loadout menu and the pause menu"). [API]
- Movement: mouse-wheel adjusts **movement speed** continuously; faster and heavier means louder (PCGamesN: "You can adjust Gideon's movement speed with the scroll wheel, but the faster you move, and the heavier the equipment you're carrying, the more noise you'll make" [PCGN prologue article]). Going prone while standing still makes no noise; the faster you move, the faster you can drop prone but the more noise it makes (IV2 devlog). Making noise inside a closet was a bug (IV2 1.2.9: "fixed being able to make noise while inside a closet"). [API]
- **Ambient noise** is a first-class number: "Make noise" action (mic and button) takes ambient noise into account but never exceeds it (IV2 Patch 1.0.2). **Rain** raises ambient noise "both inside and outside of buildings (more outside than inside)", so you can be noisier unheard; rain exists on 5 base campaign levels in IV1 and mutators force or forbid it (IV1 1.3.0). Indoor sounds are now muffled (IV2 1.4.0). [API]
- **Noise propagation**: "walls dampen noise" (Borderline 1.1.11 "fixed walls incorrectly influencing noise travel (they should've dampened noise more)"); 1.1.17 reworked "how goons track the player through walls when he makes noise - they will now instead look to the shortest path through which noise came through, instead of tracking the noise emitter through walls". The noise system can be CPU-heavy. A noise-propagation bug ("incorrect reactions") and stale noise persisting across sessions were fixed (1.1.1, 1.3.0). [API]
- **Weapons**: every weapon has a "Shot noise" in metres (screens: S&W 457S with suppressor mod loadout: 98 M base [SHOT iv2_1]; Scorpion EVO 3: 34 M [SHOT iv3_2]); suppressors give about -20% shot noise (screen) and subsonic ammo helps ("Increased noise reduction when used with subsonic ammo"); .45 ACP weapons with regular ammo are NOT subsonic (IV2 1.1.3 fix), UMP/MAC-10 in .45 ACP got a slight reduction in shot noise (1.2.3). Gunfire noise has a **slider in custom difficulty** (IV1 1.3.0). Gas grenade explosion noise was reduced (IV2 1.1). Explosions sound different indoors and outdoors (IV2 1.4.0). Holstered weapons add movement noise ("Noise increase (holstered) +7%/+25%" in the stat panel). [API][SHOT]
- Knife kills: body fall makes noise; knife kill duration 1.5 s reduced to 1.1 s (IV2 1.2.0). Dropped throwables, empty magazines and thrown knives are **noticed by enemies on social-stealth levels** (crash fix in IV2 1.1.6 shows this). Noisemaker (remote-controlled lure; enemies pocket it if you leave it, "a novelty to them"), thrown bottles/magazines are distractions. Thrown objects at an enemy in combat no longer force him back to alert (Borderline 1.1.2). [API]
- Eavesdropping: nearby guards' dialogue is shown as text; out of range it says "[Unintelligible, out of earshot]" (Borderline 1.1.14). Eavesdrop states have dedicated sounds (1.3.0) and a HUD "EAVESDROPPING..." toast [SHOT iv3_0]. Pickup markers appear for items the dialogue mentions (1.1.3). [API]
- Sound **display**: noise meter in HUD, per-item small bar icons in the weapon/gadget list (screen iv2_3 [SHOT]); "Times heard" end-screen stat counts only when a goon or pedestrian actually becomes suspicious or alert (IV2 1.4.3). [API]

### 1.3 Line of sight, doors, bodies, hiding

- Enemies see by cone and line of sight; garage doors that were see-through at the edges let enemies spot you through them (IV2 1.1.1 fix); windows block or allow sight; "goons staring" is its own behaviour ("stare" pathfinds, "angle holding" in combat). [API]
- **Closets/cupboards**: hide in them; if nobody sees you enter it is safe; if you are seen entering you get "a moment's notice to get out before you're peppered with lead" (IV2 devlog). Actions are blocked inside (weapon switching inside caused a lost-weapon bug, Borderline 1.2.0). [API]
- **Bodies**: carry them (drag, with a ghost preview hint "Back S W Forward" [SHOT iv2_7]), drop in **dumpsters** (counts as a kill for objective purposes, IV2 1.3.15), stack them (IV2 1.2.1), choke-hold then keep holding to grab the body (IV1 1.2). Body detection speed was halved in IV2 1.2.0, and fixes show enemies mark bodies as "seen" when seeing them, then react. Dead and unconscious bodies are slightly darkened so they blend in (IV1 1.2). Bodies are ignored for pickup targeting while any enemy is in combat (IV1 1.4.0.1). Unconscious enemies can wake with the "Waking enemies" mutator. Blood on the ground is a clue: enemy line "What the fuck... is that blood?" [SHOT bd_0]; "fixed snapping a goon's neck leaving blood" (Borderline 1.0.1). Carrying a body used to trigger goons to react to the body rather than you (Borderline 1.1.13 fix). [API]
- **Doors**: enemies react when hit by a door that is opened into them (Borderline 1.3.0 fix list, 1.1.15). Door interaction by scroll-wheel object menu (IV1 style, returned as option in IV2 1.1). Lockpicking vs shooting the lock: "Bring the right equipment to lockpick a door, and you'll save yourself the risk of being heard breaking it open" [PCGN prologue article]; "shoot out the next door lock" (IV1 store text). [API]
- **Social stealth levels**: a few levels have civilians/pedestrians and visible-weapon rules: "concealable" weapons (Mini-Uzi, MP7, MAC-10) can be carried without alarming; pedestrians who go missing can be noticed by goons (IV2 1.2.0 removed this for residences). [API]
- **Disguise**: no classic disguise system found; the "social stealth" concealable-weapon rule is the closest. Some police-looking enemies are disguised goons (COMM achievements guide).

### 1.4 Detection meters and grace

- Detection is time-based: lighting level, distance and "how quickly" you are spotted (IV2 devlog). IV2 1.2.8: "tweaked the rate at which enemies detect the player when they are not alert (it is now a little slower)". Borderline 1.0.x: Easy difficulty goons had incorrectly reacted as fast as True (1.1.1 fix), so reaction time scales by difficulty. [API]
- Overhead indicators: "?!" display when a laser alarm trips an alarm a goon is monitoring (Borderline 1.1.4); "not visible" overhead displays when prone (1.2.0 fix); red target marker above an enemy being aimed at [SHOT iv3_3]. A level-end **stealth rating** is computed from times spotted, times heard, and kills; "the 'enemies killed' stat will now influence the stealth rating only if the enemy has at some point seen the player or was alert" (IV2 1.4.1). [API]
- IV3 noise reaction model (Devlog #1, 17 Jul 2026), the most explicit design statement: in IV1 enemies reacted to noise instantly. Now reaction time depends on three things: **noise radius** (quieter noise = slower reaction), **enemy alertness state** (relaxed reacts slowly, suspicious or alert reacts quicker, combat as fast as before) and **enemy stress level** (every heard or seen player event raises stress, which "can completely outweigh the other two parameters"). Also: loud, far or wall-obscured noise still alerts but enemies only learn the **general area, not the exact position**. [API]

## 2. Enemy AI

### 2.1 States
Explicit names: **relaxed/idle**, **suspicious**, **alert**, **combat** (devlog #1), plus scene states such as "startled" (interrupting a scene by knocking a participant out puts goons into "startled", not combat, Borderline 1.0.2), "run to weapon in fear" (IV2 1.1.4), "avoid smoke", "run from grenade", "eavesdrop-able chatter", "stare/watch". The mod API exposes `npcAlertnessStates` (IV2 1.4.6). Music mirrors this: idle / suspicion / alert tracks crossfade with proximity (Borderline 1.0.4, 1.1.14). [API]

### 2.2 Perception and reactions
IV1 store text: "If they've found a dead body they will alert their friends in hopes of finding you. If they hear you sneaking about, they will investigate. If they see you, they will open fire. If they're unarmed, they will try to find their friends and warn them of you." Enemies "take cover during gunfights, use flashlights in darkness, and radio in suspicious activity". [API]

- **Radios**: goons carry radios; watching **security cameras** they notify others over radio (crash fix IV2 1.1: "a lot of enemies on security cameras would notify other enemies of the same intruder over the radio"); camera watchers stay in place instead of following allies (Borderline 1.0.8). Dead radio owners' "radio on" sounds were bugs fixed, implying radio-on audio cues exist. Reinforcements spawn at map points ("NPC reinforcement spawn points"); reinforcement spawns get flashlights and radios (IV2 1.4.0). "Enemies refuse to move" bug fixed by rewriting the pathfind queue; up to 16 pathfinder worker threads (IV2 1.1). [API]
- **Alerting others**: nearby goons become alert when one sees a dead ally (Borderline 1.1.16 fix: "goons reacting to dead allies sometimes not triggering nearby goons to also become alert"). Back-kills or chokes on a scene participant alert the other participants (IV2 1.2.0).
- **Lights out / switched on**: see 1.1. **Smoke detectors, sprinklers, fire alarms** exist (IV2 optimisation notes; achievements guide "floor 27 has no fire alarms"). Fire is avoided during pathing (Borderline 1.1.4: "goons will now avoid fire when pathfinding").
- **Grenades**: goons throw grenades and flee from thrown ones ("fixed goons sometimes not running away from grenades", 1.0.4; "even if they can't pathfind to a point to hide", 1.0.7); you can shoot thrown grenades to detonate them immediately (1.0.2). Smoke now travels through vents and open windows and puts out fires (Borderline 1.0.1, 1.0.2, 1.3.0). Goons blind-fire into smoke when the player is obscured (1.1.0 fix). [API]
- **Weapon spread** and recoil apply equally to NPCs ("NPCs also use the exact same weapon spread system", IV1 1.4.0); in IV2 1.1.2/1.2.0 the dev found enemies had easier controllability than the player and fixed it. Enemy weapons get mods in late campaign levels (IV2 1.2.0). [API]

### 2.3 Combat behaviours (Borderline 1.3.0, 5 Nov 2026, the best AI source)
New goon combat AI: **FLANK** (in dangerous killzones), **SIEGE** (hold cover around the camp spot instead of far away), **GROUP BREACH** (several goons advance, wait for each other, opportunistic flashbang, then all rush together), **grenade flush-outs** of a camping player, **SUPPRESSION** (clear line of sight fires at the camp spot so allies can move), **ANGLE HOLDING** (hold the last line of sight while allies move), **LEAPFROG ADVANCE** (cover allies as they move cover to cover), **FALL BACK and REGROUP** (when several are taken down in quick succession). Also "killzone prevention" is a saved state (reset-to-save fix), and "goons mistaking their own allies' fire for the player's" was fixed. Goons trip and fall when shot while climbing (1.1.0), have a short window when tripped to take more bullets (IV2 1.4.0), and their weapons fly out of their hands on death. [API]

### 2.4 Difficulty
Preset names seen: **Easy**, normal, **Hard**, **True**, **Throwback** (easier damage/bandage), **Masochist/Masochism** (insta-death; "hurt" effect shows even if body armour saved you) and a **Custom** difficulty with many sliders: infinite savefiles, save difficulty factors ("Money pickups per save", "Milestone savefiles"), gunfire noise, armor protection, "Notice dropped weapons", weapon/flashlight/enemy-experience randomisation, and "Enemies always alert" mutator. Reaction speed differs per difficulty. Difficulty can be changed mid-playthrough in IV2 (lower difficulty locks the higher achievements). [API][COMM]

### 2.5 How it is built (developer statements)
- Pathfinding on a grid with worker threads; tile occupancy (`actorTileOccupancy`) and a quadtree; "stare pathfinds" computed with jump point search (JPS+) and a greedy search for up to 2.4x then 3.1x speed; region and JPS+ maps shared between worker threads; the grid is updated when objects (bushes, doors) change (Borderline 1.1.10, 1.1.17, 1.1.18). Bushes mark the grid so goons avoid them. [API]
- "Noise propagation system" is a separate system that tracks stale noise events (Borderline 1.3.0). AI cover search and "cover stare logic" are named components (IV2 1.4.8, 1.1.5). [API]
- Design philosophy quote (Devlog #1): the series is "filled with little details that, when isolated, seem like nothing special at all. It is when they're put together, that the jigsaw puzzle ... presents itself." [API]

## 3. Player kit

### 3.1 Weapons (names seen in patch notes, store text and screenshots)
Store claim IV2: **29 weapons, 17 gadgets**, "a range of ammo types", mods such as suppressors and laser sights. [API] The set named across patch notes: Mini-Uzi, MP5(N), MP7, UMP (.40 S&W, convertible to 9x19 and .45 ACP, 600 RPM), MAC-10 (.45 ACP, 1,090 RPM, 1,300 in 9x19, "hard to control during continuous full-auto fire"), FAMAS G2 (5.56x45, 1,100 RPM), G36C, AR-15 (burst), AK-103/AK-107, AS VAL, HK417 (one-shots most targets per community), Remington Model 700 bolt rifle (4x scope added 1.2.0), SF19 (7.62x51), Tranquilizer Rifle, Multi-Launcher, Scorpion EVO 3, Vector 10mm, shotguns Remington 870, Browning Auto-5, M1014, Saiga-12K (drum mag and slugs), Sawn-off, pistols P89, P320, Glock-18C, Glock-20 (10mm), MK4 (.22 LR), HS2000 (.40), S&W 457S, MK23, VP9, .44 Revolver (Borderline), Supporter Pack: Five-seveN (5.7x28, 20 rd), M1911 (.45, 7 rd), P90 (50 rd), DP-12 (double-barrel 12 ga, 14 rd). [API][COMM]

**Stats shown in the UI** (screens): total damage (e.g. 42.8 with a bracketed base), penetration rating (4 on Scorpion EVO 3), bullet speed (251 and 419 m/s, falls in flight to a minimum of 66% of muzzle velocity, IV2 1.2.0 beta 27), rate of fire (500, 1150), magazine capacity, reload speed %, accuracy in degrees, shot deviation, accuracy recovery, aim zoom range, pellet group spread, spread-per-shot, controllability %, max additive spread, turn accuracy instability, max turn spread, mobile accuracy, focus accuracy boost and speed, shot noise in metres, holstered noise increase, slowdown. [SHOT iv2_1, iv3_2] Rules from patch notes: the spread system is highly dynamic: continuous fire, movement speed, mouse movement all add spread (IV1 1.4.0); weapon skills raise both spread recovery and "controllability" (rate of spread growth in sustained fire, IV2 1.2.0); muzzle velocity deviates slightly each shot; visual recoil/camera shake is light "since vision is a very important aspect of combat" (IV1 1.4.0). Per-shot ammo state is tracked, staged reloads (mag out, mag in, bolt release; a reload can be cancelled by interacting or meleeing and resumed from the last stage; IV2 1.4.0), **tactical reload** drops a part-full magazine on the floor and saves up to 0.3 s (IV1 Dev Update #2), magazine system mutator (manual magazine loading). Unload nearby weapons/pick up ammo is an action; carried ammo per caliber is capped and shown by a filling icon. [API]

### 3.2 Attachments, mods, ammo
Mod categories: magazine (extended, small, drum: screen "+20 mag capacity, -20% reload speed, -10% turn accuracy stability, +8% noise when holstered" [SHOT iv3_2]), barrel (threaded, match barrel, compensator, long-port suppressor, suppressor and "suppressor NE"), sights (glow sights, 2x, 4x), trigger and trigger groups (binary trigger, burst trigger group, full-auto trigger on Auto-5, burst on M1014), recoil spring, foregrip, laser pointer, caliber conversion mods (10mm and 9mm for MP5, 9mm for Vector). Each mod lists signed percentages for damage per shot, bullet speed, shot deviation, accuracy recovery, spread, max additive spread, focus, turn accuracy, mobile accuracy, shot noise, movement noise, movement speed. Mods cost money in the loadout shop (screen prices $150-$900) and are **unlocked by exfiltrating with a modded weapon** (IV2 1.2.0). Presets can be saved. **Ammo types**: subsonic, .40 S&W Match, #00 buckshot, slugs, beanbag shotgun rounds (non-lethal, IV2 1.1), plus Multi-Launcher shells (rubber, EMP, gas). [API][SHOT]

### 3.3 Armour, health, healing
Vests: kevlar, ceramic plate, **kevlar + plate** combo, plate carrier; plates can be swapped or removed; broken kevlar loses its plate; armour has a flat reduction and "additional damage dampening the further the bullet is from penetrating" (IV1 1.4.0). Penetration versus armour is compared per weapon ("increased MP7 armor penetration value"). Armour affects carry capacity (Load Bearing skill gives extra magazines if wearing armour), noise and mobility (loadout meters). Healing: bandages (use limit per level; not on Throwback), **Medkit** heals 40 HP in total and doesn't count against the bandage limit (IV1 1.4.0), painkillers, an **inhaler** that triggers slow-motion (IV2 1.2.0). Death returns to the last savefile or "reset to last save". [API]

### 3.4 Gadgets and throwables
Flashbang, frag grenade (blast wave visual, can be shot to detonate), gas grenade (non-lethal, first of its kind in IV2; "be careful not to inhale"), smoke grenade (exists: "smoke now travels through vent shafts", spreads through open windows, extinguishes fires), Molotov cocktail (fire with flamelets and oil barrels that leak and ignite; thrown knives hit petrol barrels and make them leak), flares (keep their thrown colour), motion sensor mine, **Taser Mine** (remotely detonated; a goon may pick it up and carry it so you can trigger it later), **Noisemaker** (remote-controlled lure), taser (handheld), throwing knife (auto-swaps with a gadget when slots are full), **HSR-EMP**, **PKC-EMP**, NVGs, medkit, security-camera monitor use, lockpick, heavy throwables that knock goons out (Borderline 1.0.x crash fix "knocking out a goon with a heavy throwable"), eavesdrop. Gadget slots are limited (error "no free gadget slots"). Throwing is a mode: you can cook or prepare a throw; you cannot go prone mid-throw (fixed dupe bug). [API]

### 3.5 Melee, takedowns, movement
Melee strike, **back kill**, **choke** (hold the key to keep grabbing the body), **knife kill** (1.1 s), punches and kicks (ceiling lamps can block kicks, Borderline 1.0.1), door bash that hits enemies, "finish off" knocked-down enemies by aiming at them; "Hand-to-hand" skill. Pickpocket and "unload" actions exist on controller (Borderline 1.0.8). Movement: mouse-wheel speed from crawl to run, a **sprint** perk with a sprint bar ("Sprinter"), crouch/prone, window climbing and vaulting (limited to marked climb points in IV1/IV2; most objects in IV3), vent shafts entered at an angle with an up/down movement rule, lean is not mentioned in any source (I found no lean). Time slow: Sean's **bullet time / stim** (becoming addicted is an achievement "Stim Junkie"); Steve has a **kill-triggered slo-mo** ("Steve's kill slo-mo is now slightly easier to trigger", 1.3.11). Slo-mo causes vomit/blur side effects after (Borderline 1.1.12). [API][COMM]

### 3.6 Skills, perks, progression
Weapon and action skills level by use ("increased the amount of experience given when firing and reloading weapons", "Short Gun Reload", "Hand-to-hand"): XP thresholds for level 1 and level 3 are 150 and 3,250 (IV2 1.4.0). A **Learning Capacity** pool limits how many skill sub-levels you can buy; New Game+ gives +3,000 (was 2,000) per playthrough; skills can be reset; **perk points** buy perks (Sprinter, Load Bearing). Steve can **train** you for 1,000 money; **Trust** (% with Steve; side missions give up to 40% total) drives endings; killing 10 civilians or low trust yields the bad ending; mercy (sparing three targets) shapes the best ending. Money buys weapons/ammo; Volatile Weapons mutator removes un-exfiltrated weapons. [API][COMM]

## 5. Graphics and atmosphere

### 5.1 How it looks and is rendered
- **Top-down 2D pixel art** with a tile grid ("tilegrid rendering", floor tile cluster animation), sprite sheets repacked with sprite deduping and NPOT sizing (-120 MB RAM, -100 MB VRAM, Borderline 1.1.8). Props are heavily dressed; blood and bullet decals persist (blood pools in [SHOT iv1_12, bd_0]); casings, rain droplets (can be lit or unlit), shell sounds. Walls can be broken and rebuilt ("rebuilding walls once they'd be broken", 1.1.0), doors and puddles are objects with their own cost. [API][SHOT]
- **Dynamic lighting with real shadows**: every lamp, flashlight and camera is a light caster with a cone and cone fall-off (IV3:B 1.1.2 "fixed varying cone fall-off values"); a **shadow-mapping system** rendering to atlases with quality levels (Normal/Soft/Ultra filtering; shadow quality affects Fog of War); **fake shadows** that stack (object B casts onto A) (IV1 1.4.0); **cloud shadows** moving over outdoor areas; **static lighting** baked with "tile visibility baking" including cameras and a visibility-grid compute shader; lighting **culling** (up to 25% win); **RTGI and bounce lighting** (Borderline 1.1.3, 1.1.15) plus **LBAO** (cone-traced ambient occlusion with ray jittering; medium looks like old very high); **depth-aware lighting** to remove halos; HDR pipeline with bloom, exposure that follows the cursor ("HDR exposure no longer follows the cursor when a menu is open"), tone mapping (default and 'filmic', plus 'High-contrast rendering' blend mode), reduced banding in dark areas. Light flashes (muzzle flash, explosions, fires) are light sources; fire is a cluster of "flamelets" which also drives smoke detector cost. Gas, smoke and sprinklers are simulated with spread through vents, windows, walls and sprinklers fade gas faster. [API]
- **Light colour language** [SHOT]: warm amber interiors, cold blue-white flashlight cones, green industrial/medical rooms, red camera/laser fans, magenta and cyan neon nightclub with checkerboard tiles, yellow vehicle lights, near-black ambient with soft vignette. PC Gamer on the prologue: "The whole color palette is dull greys and browns, with flickering lights unveiling a lot of color at times while masking events at others... Warm lamplight and overheads contrast with blinding LEDs and flickering fluorescent tubes" [PCG]. NVG is green grain/teal with bloom [SHOT].
- **UI**: bottom-left HUD cluster (health bar with armour pip, light and noise meters, weapon slot with ammo, stance icon), vertical weapon/gadget selector with noise icons, minimal objective tracker top-left ("Neutralize everyone (1/24)"), floating enemy speech text and a left dialogue box with pixel portraits, object-interaction menu (scroll wheel or list), point-of-interest markers that fade when the mouse is over them, a custom cursor/crosshair, line-of-sight line/cone, red target marker, outline on headshot-aimed goon, HUD "?!" markers. Menus blur the game behind (blurred loadout screens [SHOT iv2_1, iv2_6]). Ultrawide: UI stays centred as in 16:9 (Borderline 1.1.7). [API][SHOT]
- **Audio**: dynamic score by Xtrullor, "stealth ambient vs action" polar opposites; idle/suspicion/alert variants crossfade by distance; separate idle-music volume slider (Borderline 1.1.11); stingers for "everyone neutralized" and "everyone dead" (IV2 1.4.0); indoor muffling; weapon-specific pitch variance; shell casing sounds; wet footsteps on puddles; rain; goons' spoken-text lines (displayed as text bubbles, e.g. "Why don't you come out, pussy!" [SHOT]); PCGamesN: "things will remain quiet while stealthing, punchy tracks will kick in as soon as you've been fully detected" [PCGN IV2 launch article]. ## 10. What our game should take from Intravenous

Adapted to a side-view, three.js infiltration game.

Stealth and light
1. Show two always-on meters (light level and noise) in the HUD, and make both continuous values rather than on/off flags. Detection time then derives from them. [Under the Guise of Darkness]
2. Darkness never equals invisibility: use `visibleDistance = f(lightLevel)` and `spotTime = g(lightLevel, distance)`, with a tiny "bump into you" radius that always detects. [IV2 devlog]
3. Compute light level from the **grayscale luminance** of the light at the player's position, not the sum of colours, so coloured but dim neon does not light you up. [IV1 1.4.0]
4. Give light a **height/layer** dimension so crates, counters and bushes (in side view: foreground props, shadows from catwalks) can shade you from a lamp. [IV2 1.4.0 cover from lamps]
5. Every lamp has an explicit controller: shootable, wall switch, room breaker box or building fuse box. Store the links in level data, and add an editor warning for unlinked lamps (the dev spent patches fixing this). [IV2 patches]
6. Make guards react to lights: complain ("Damn lights..."), walk to the switch or fuse box, repair broken breakers, and react when you turn lights ON as well. Don't let them keep trying forever (the loop bugs). [IV2 1.3.2, Borderline 1.1.15]
7. Broken lamps spark and flicker, EMP-disrupted lamps can be re-disrupted, EMP charge regenerates (even when dropped). Include a short-range area EMP that is heavy and noisy and an aimed single-target EMP. [IV1 1.4.0, Borderline 1.3.0]
8. Draw enemy flashlights as soft-edged cones; cameras as red fans with visible min/max sweep angles; the player's own view cone as a faint fan. Use soft fall-off and fade-in. [IV1 1.4.0, IV2 1.2.3]
9. Add a faint player **silhouette** when in deep darkness so the player never loses themselves, while the AI still treats it as dark. [IV2 1.2.0]
10. NVG as a toggle post-effect: desaturated teal/green, film grain, strong bloom on lights; keep it from being fully green. [IV1 1.4.0, shots]
11. LOS indicator with 3 options (regular, limited, minimal) and a laser-pointer variant that raises your visibility. [IV2 1.2.0, Borderline 1.1.17]

Sound
12. Every weapon and gadget has a shot-noise radius in metres with suppressor and subsonic ammo modifiers, a holstered noise modifier and a movement-noise modifier. Show these numbers in the loadout screen. [IV2 shots]
13. A continuous movement-speed control (scroll wheel in the original; in side view, hold-to-walk with a slider or analogue) where speed and carried weight scale footstep noise. Prone/stationary stance changes make zero noise. [IV2 devlog]
14. **Ambient noise level** as a global (rain, machinery, nightclub music, generators) that masks the player's noise: sound only counts above ambient, and "make noise" distractions are capped at ambient. Rain louder outside than in. [IV1 1.3.0, IV2 1.0.2]
15. Propagate noise through the level graph/walls with damping; AI heads along the **shortest path the noise travelled**, not straight through the wall; far or wall-muffled noise only reveals a general area, not an exact position. [Borderline 1.1.17, Devlog #1]
16. AI reaction delay = f(noise radius, alertness state, stress level); stress rises with each heard/seen event and can override the other two. No instant reactions to quiet noise. [Devlog #1]
17. Eavesdropping: show guards' dialogue as text in range, "[Unintelligible, out of earshot]" out of range, and place markers on items they mention (codes, keycards). [Borderline 1.1.14, 1.1.3]
18. Distractions: thrown bottles/mags, a remote noisemaker that guards pocket if left alone, and a taser mine that a guard can carry in his pocket until you detonate it. [IV2 devlog]

AI and alarms
19. Explicit states relaxed, suspicious, alert, combat, with scene states (startled, eavesdrop-able chatter, watching camera) layered on top; music tracks follow the state and crossfade by distance of the nearest threat. [IV2, Borderline 1.1.14]
20. Bodies, blood pools and open doors are clues; sighted bodies alert nearby goons; halve detection speed on bodies for fairness; allow dumpsters/lockers/vents for hiding bodies. [IV2 1.2.0, Borderline 1.1.16]
21. Radios: guards carrying radios broadcast when they spot you or a body (visible "radio on" cue); killing the radio carrier quickly or cutting it with EMP/jamming denies reinforcements; camera watchers radio you in. [IV2 1.1]
22. Combat AI built from named tactics: flank, siege around the camp spot, group breach with flashbang, grenade flush-outs, suppression fire, angle holding, leapfrog advance, fall back and regroup after several losses. Gate each behind a "killzone" evaluation of where the player is camping. [Borderline 1.3.0]
23. Enemies avoid fire and run from thrown grenades; they blind-fire into smoke; you can shoot a thrown grenade to detonate it early; smoke extinguishes fire, travels through vents and open windows. [Borderline 1.0.2, 1.1.4, 1.3.0]
24. Reinforcement spawns at map points that come with flashlights and radios; AI trips and drops weapons when shot while climbing. [IV2 1.4.0, Borderline 1.1.0]
25. Run pathfinding in workers with a shared, incrementally updated navigation graph; use JPS-like search for line-of-sight "stare" targets, and recompute only when doors or props change. In three.js, use Web Workers. [Borderline 1.1.17-1.1.18]

Kit and progression
26. Loadout as a stat-sheet with signed percentages per mod (damage, speed, spread, noise, mobility) and a "Noise factor" and "Mobility" total for the whole kit; unlock mods by exfiltrating with them. [IV2 shots, 1.2.0]
27. Dynamic spread (continuous fire, movement speed, aim movement) shared between player and AI, with light visual recoil; staged reloads that can be interrupted and resumed. [IV1 1.4.0, IV2 1.4.0]
28. Armour as plates plus vest with partial damage reduction by distance from penetration; armour adds noise and reduces mobility. [IV1 1.4.0, shots]
29. Non-lethal options matched with lethal: gas, taser, beanbag, tranquilizer, choke; a "neutralised" versus "dead" count that feeds the stealth rating and stingers. [IV2, 1.4.0]
30. Slow-motion "bullet time" with side effects (blur, vomit, addiction), plus an inhaler that triggers it. [IV2 1.2.0, Borderline 1.1.12]
31. Skills that level by use, a Learning Capacity pool, perks (sprint, load-bearing), and trust meters driving endings. [IV2 1.4.0, COMM]
32. Mutators and custom difficulty (fog of war, enemies always alert, volatile weapons, limited saves) to extend replay. [IV1 1.3.0]

Level design and scoring
33. Give each mission 3-5 routes: vents, windows (climbable vs barred), garage doors, lockpick/keycard/shoot-the-lock, fuse box. Mark them with clear outlines (vent outlines, climb points). [IV2, Borderline 1.1.14]
34. Keycards bound to doors, multiple per level; objectives like "Neutralize everyone (n/N)", "exfiltrate undetected"; stealth rating from times spotted, times heard and kills of enemies that actually saw you. [IV2 1.4.1]
35. Side-view adaptations: vent and ladder shafts become explicit layers; lamps cast cones that include floor/ceiling height; fuse boxes live on specific floors; floors are separated by stairs and elevators with their own noise propagation; camera cones sweep in 2D with visible arcs.

Presentation
36. Grungy palette: near-black ambient with warm amber interiors, cold blue-white flashlights, green industrial/medical rooms, red camera/laser, magenta/cyan neon clubs, plus flickering fluorescents. Build with additive coloured 2D lights and a normal-map-less sprite look. [PCG, shots]
37. Render pipeline checklist (adapt to three.js): 2D shadow map per light (or a visibility polygon), baked static light visibility with a dynamic overlay for moving lights, stacking fake shadows for props, cloud shadows outdoors, cheap bounce light/AO as optional quality tiers, HDR with bloom and a tone-map with a filmic and a high-contrast mode, and dither to reduce banding in dark areas. [Borderline 1.1.0, 1.1.8, IV1 1.3-1.4]
38. Fire, smoke, gas as simulated fields with spread through vents, windows, and sprinklers; smoke detectors and sprinklers react (disruptable by EMP). [IV2, Borderline 1.0.1, 1.3.0]
39. HUD: bottom-left cluster (health with armour pip, light and noise meters, weapon, stance icon), selector with noise icons, a left dialogue box with portraits, fading POI markers, "?!" markers above monitored alarms. [shots]
40. Music with three layers (idle, suspicion, alert) crossfaded by proximity plus separate idle-volume slider, stingers when everyone is neutralised or dead, and muffling of indoor sounds. [IV2 1.4.0, Borderline 1.0.4, 1.1.11]
41. Speech bubbles: enemies shout lines as floating text, reacting to the situation (blood, darkness, smoke); don't repeat lines (a bug where lines never reappeared was fixed). [shots, Borderline 1.1.16]
42. Keep performance budgets honest: solo-dev engine runs these lights on Intel Iris class GPUs at 1080p 60 fps. [IV2 requirements]
